How to play

  • Free tile — nothing stacked on top, and at least one of left or right side open.
  • Match — tap two free tiles with the same geometric face to remove them.
  • Hint — highlights one valid free pair (first few free, then rewarded stub).
  • Shuffle — rearranges remaining faces in place when you are stuck.
  • Win — clear every tile on the layout.
