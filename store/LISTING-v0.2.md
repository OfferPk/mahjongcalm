**Full description**

MahjongCalm is a quiet tile-matching solitaire. Pick two free tiles with the same face to clear them, and empty the board to finish the layout. The tiles use original geometric faces, so they're easy to read at a glance.

★ 12 layouts, from Calm Turtle to Meadow
★ Undo, Redo, Shuffle and Hint whenever you need them
★ 3 stars for a clean clear without spare moves or help
★ Daily reward calendar (7 days) and a new Daily challenge every day
★ Skin Shop: tile sets and table backdrops. Tap to preview, or try any skin free for 24 hours
★ Achievements and a Stats page
★ Tutorial on your first layout and a How to play card
★ Reduce motion, larger text and high contrast options
★ Fully offline

Fair play: coins are cosmetic only. They're earned by playing and can never be bought or cashed out. Ads are optional rewarded videos you choose to watch (extra hints, 24h skin trials), plus an occasional ad between layouts after your first session. Never on launch.
